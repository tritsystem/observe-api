Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: August 2026

OBSERVE Search API sells prepaid credits, not physical goods, so "returns" don't apply in the usual sense — this policy covers refunds.

Unused credits

If you purchased credits and haven't used any of them, contact us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

Partially used credits

If you've used some but not all of a credit purchase, contact us within 14 days of purchase — refunds for the unused portion are considered on a case-by-case basis (credits already spent on completed searches or private indexing runs reflect real compute cost already incurred and are not refundable).

Billing errors

If you believe you were charged incorrectly (e.g., a duplicate charge, or a search that failed but still deducted a credit — failed searches are supposed to auto-refund their credit, so this would indicate a bug), contact us and we'll investigate and correct it.

How to request a refund

Contact the address listed on the homepage with your API key's associated email and the Stripe payment/session ID from your receipt. Refunds are issued back to the original payment method via Stripe.
